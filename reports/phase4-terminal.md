# Terminal maintenance: bounded diagnosis

The four existing v8 COK loss replays, seeds 3029 and 3063 in both seats, do
not substantiate the proposed avoidable-feed mechanism. During days 24–29,
**zero successful FEED actions** targeted an animal with neither a future
saleable primary event nor held product. The incumbent already tests both
conditions. None of these replays fed on the terminal day.

| Seed | Seat | Successful FEED | Successful CARE | CARE with no saleable bonus | Feed protecting held product after final event |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3029 | 0 | 80 | 69 | 19 | 9 |
| 3029 | 1 | 76 | 68 | 16 | 5 |
| 3063 | 0 | 78 | 74 | 18 | 3 |
| 3063 | 1 | 78 | 74 | 18 | 3 |

The CARE defect is real: the interpreter produces first, consumes pending
care, and only then adds today's care to the next cycle. Care on the final
production transition cannot improve a deliverable primary product. Nine
official refresh probes cover all three animal species and production dates
28, 29 and 30. Crucially, **production appearing on observation day 29 remains
saleable**; it is generated by the end-of-day transition on day 28. Removing
all maintenance once an event exceeds day 28 would incorrectly sacrifice it.
Production on day 30 is unavailable before the last action, day 29 hour 22.

Additional official probes verify that an unfed animal with an existing
one-day feeding deficit escapes and destroys four held units, while feeding
preserves them. Thus a feed with no remaining new primary event is not by
itself waste. Daily manure also makes a primary-only maintenance rule
economically incomplete.

The demonstrated avoidable work is 16–19 CARE actions per game, with **zero
direct input cost**. No replay counterfactual establishes that freeing those
actions earns more than 500 cash or permits fewer hired workers. Travel savings
and alternative harvest opportunities would depend on the resulting schedule;
assigning an arbitrary cash-per-action rate would not establish realized value.
Consequently this bounded hypothesis does **not** meet the requested materiality
criterion. No candidate or new games are warranted on this evidence. Keep the
calendar correction as a low-cost follow-up if a future scheduler already
models alternative action value, rather than presenting it as a competitive
breakthrough.

Reproduce the official probes and successful-action reconstruction:

```console
python scripts/phase4_terminal.py --output reports/results/phase4-terminal-audit.json.gz
python -m pytest tests/eval/test_phase4_terminal.py -q
```

The compact audit records artifact/interpreter/replay hashes and individual
unproductive care actions. It uses original observations and actions solely
for offline diagnosis, and makes no new stochastic game transitions. Replays
remain in the ignored `reports/replays/phase4-losses` directory; the committed
audit preserves the measured evidence when those large files are absent.
